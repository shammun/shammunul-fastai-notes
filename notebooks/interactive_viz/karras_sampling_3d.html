How to read the mountain

Every purple ball is one sample being generated. Its height tells you its current σ; its floor position is its current guess in data space. The dashed vertical drop under the hero ball is its "shadow" — where it currently sits in data space if you ignore the noise axis.

orange cloud = pure-noise starting points at σ_max · rings = the Karras rungs (spacing shrinks downward!) · purple balls = samples, hero ball is bigger & orange-rimmed · teal discs = data clusters on the floor · red ghost (stage ⑤) = uncorrected Euler for the hero · purple flag = the model's clean-guess D(x,σ)
