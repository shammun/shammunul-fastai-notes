Convolution = one big matrix multiplication (im2col / unfold)

Nested Python loops are slow. The trick: ① rip the image into all its 3×3 patches, ② lay each patch out as a column of a matrix, ③ flatten the kernel into a row and do one matmul, ④ reshape the result back into the output image. Click any pipeline stage, click a matrix column to trace patch ↔ output, or press Play.
