❌ Naive: log(sum(exp(x)))
✅ Trick: m + log(sum(exp(x − m)))

The trick works because x − m ≤ 0 for every element, so every exp() lands safely in (0, 1]. We pull the big number m outside the exp where it is just harmless addition. Mathematically nothing changed — it's the same shift-invariance you saw in the softmax playground.
