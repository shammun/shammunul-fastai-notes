Stage 1. A Fashion-MNIST image is a 28×28 grid of integer brightness values (0 = black, 255 = white). Here is an 8×8 boot pictogram standing in for it. Press Step to feed it through the pipeline.

img # PIL.Image, 28×28, mode 'L'

TF.to_tensor does two jobs at once: it scales pixels ÷255 into floats in [0,1], and it reorders axes from H×W×C to C×H×W (channels-first). torch.flatten then collapses everything into a 1-D vector — only needed for non-convolutional (linear) models; conv nets keep the 2-D shape.
