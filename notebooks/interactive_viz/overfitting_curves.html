Why you must watch the validation set

Drag epochs trained. Training loss (orange) always falls — the model can memorize. Validation loss (teal) tells the truth about new data.

The gap between the curves is the generalization gap. The teal curve's lowest point is where early stopping would halt training. Bigger capacity learns faster but overfits sooner and harder — the cure is more data, regularization, or stopping in time. This is exactly why fit() evaluates on valid_dl after every epoch.
