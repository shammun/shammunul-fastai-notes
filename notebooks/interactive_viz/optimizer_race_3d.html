What to look for on this surface

Same lr for everyone is deliberately unfair to SGD — that's the lesson. RMSProp and Adam divide by the typical gradient size per parameter, so one lr works everywhere; SGD needs hand-tuning per surface. Turn on gradient noise to make it truly stochastic GD and watch momentum's averaging shine.
