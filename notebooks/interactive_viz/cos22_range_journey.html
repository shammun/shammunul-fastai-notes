Why preds[-1] * 2? One tensor, two worlds — and a unit conversion

The map on top is the whole story: the same [0, 1] tensor forks into a model world [−0.5, 0.5] (training subtracted 0.5) and a judge world [−1, 1] (the FID pipeline did ×2−1). The judge displays [−1,1] as black→white — so a raw half-range sample literally looks washed-out gray to it. That fake contrast gap inflates FID; ×2 converts the units and fixes it.
