FID = "how far apart?" + "how differently shaped?"

The Fréchet distance between two Gaussians splits into exactly two ideas. Term ① punishes clouds whose centers disagree; Term ② punishes clouds whose shapes disagree. Move the sliders (or click a term in the formula) and watch each term's bar respond — and notice how term ② goes to zero only when the ellipses match in size and orientation.
