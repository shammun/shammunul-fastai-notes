The skip-sampling race — same start, same noise, fewer UNet calls

All three strategies below denoise the same starting noise with the same randomness — the only difference is when they bother calling the UNet (purple tick = a real model call; blank = the previous ε̂ is reused for free). Time runs the way the loop does: t = 999 on the left → 0 on the right. Press ▶ Race and watch three things at once: the thumbnails sharpen, the call odometers diverge, and the red trace shows the real price of coasting — how wrong the reused ε̂ has become.
