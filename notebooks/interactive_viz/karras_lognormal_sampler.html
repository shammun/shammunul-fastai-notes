The histogram accumulates draws — note how the bell curve on the log axis becomes a lopsided “log-normal” on the real axis: most mass at medium noise, long tail to high noise, never negative.

# the one-liner from this notebook, unrolled — the highlighted line is the current stage z = torch.randn([10000]) # ① standard normal log_sigma = z * 1.2 + (-1.2) # ② scale by std, shift by mean sigma = log_sigma.exp() # ③ back to σ-space (always > 0) # equivalently: sig_samp = (torch.randn([10000]) * 1.2 - 1.2).exp()
