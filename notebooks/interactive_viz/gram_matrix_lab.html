The Gram matrix — a “fires-together” table

Style ≈ which patterns show up together, regardless of where. The Gram matrix is exactly that, as a table: entry G[c, d] asks “when detector c fires at some spot, does detector d fire at the same spot?” Big number = they co-occur a lot. Here it is at toy scale — 4 detectors on a 3×3 grid → a 4×4 table. Press ▶ Play for the guided tour, click any table cell to see it computed, and don’t miss the 🔀 shuffle proof at the end.
