Adam — and why the bias correction matters

Adam keeps both buffers: momentum's average gradient (p.avg, "which way") and RMSProp's average squared gradient (p.sqr_avg, "how big"). Both start at zero — so early in training both are biased toward zero. Dividing by 1 − βt undoes that bias exactly. Drag the t slider to see it, and walk the rail to follow the code.
