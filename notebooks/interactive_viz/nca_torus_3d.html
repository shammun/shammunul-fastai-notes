Why "circular padding"? Because the grid secretly lives on a donut 🍩

F.pad(y, [1,1,1,1], 'circular') makes the left edge neighbor the right edge and the top neighbor the bottom. That is exactly the same as gluing the edges together — fold a sheet into a cylinder, then bend the cylinder into a torus. Watch a live cellular automaton run on the sheet while you fold it: gliders that "fall off" one edge re-enter on the other, and on the torus you can see there was never an edge at all.
