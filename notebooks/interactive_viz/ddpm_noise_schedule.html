The Noise Schedule Lab: β, σ and ᾱ

Three cheap tensors — beta (noise added per step), sigma = √β, and alphabar ᾱ (total signal surviving after t steps) — control the entire diffusion process. Drag the sliders to reshape them, and click or drag on any curve to inspect a timestep t: the image preview shows exactly how noisy Fashion-MNIST looks there.

💡 Why a whole schedule and not one noise level? β_t alone looks boring — a straight line. The magic is the product: multiplying a thousand numbers that are each just barely below 1 (α_t = 1−β_t) makes ᾱ_t collapse toward ~0. That collapse must be gradual: if ᾱ dived instantly, the model would rarely practice light-noise steps; if it never reached ≈0, samples would start from “noise” that still secretly contains an image. Try β_max = 0.005 and watch the ⚠️ end-of-schedule warning — ᾱ_T stays far from 0, which silently breaks sampling.
