The stability rule behind the magic: gradient descent diverges in a valley when roughly lr > 2/curvature (a bit higher with momentum).
A sharp pit has huge curvature → small divergence threshold; a wide pit has small curvature → same lr is stable.
The high-lr phase is a filter that only sharp minima fail.
