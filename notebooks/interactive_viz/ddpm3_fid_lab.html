FID lab — measuring "how close is my fake distribution to the real one?"

FID never looks at images pixel-by-pixel. It pushes both sets through a feature extractor (a classifier with its head chopped off), fits a Gaussian (μ, Σ) to each cloud of feature vectors, and measures the distance between the two Gaussians. Here the features are 2-D so you can see everything — drag the sliders to deform the generated cloud and watch FID respond live.
