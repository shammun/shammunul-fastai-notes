√A for matrices: watch Newton–Schulz converge

FID needs √(Σ₁Σ₂) — a matrix Y such that Y·Y = A. You can't just square-root each entry! scipy.linalg.sqrtm does it on CPU (slow for 512×512); the notebook's _sqrtm_newton_schulz does it with pure matrix multiplies, so it runs on the GPU. Step through the iterations on a small matrix you can actually read, or press Play. Click any stage chip or a point on the error curve to jump.
