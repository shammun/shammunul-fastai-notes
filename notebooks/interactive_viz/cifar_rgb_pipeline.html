From Grayscale to Color: the CIFAR-10 data pipeline

Fashion MNIST images were a single grid of numbers. A CIFAR-10 image is three stacked grids — one each for Red, Green and Blue. Walk through exactly what transformi does to every image, from PIL picture to the training tensor. Hover the images to inspect real pixel values, and toggle the R / G / B channels on and off.
