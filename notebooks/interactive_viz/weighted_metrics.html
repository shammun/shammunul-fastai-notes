Why Metrics Use a Weighted Running Average

Each batch reports a loss and a count of samples. To get the true epoch loss you can't just average the batch losses — a batch of 2 shouldn't count as much as a batch of 1024. The Metric class keeps a running total ÷ total-count. Step through to add batches and compare the correct weighted mean against the naive simple mean.

In code: add(val, n) does self.vals += [val]; self.ns += [n], and the metric is (vals·ns).sum() / ns.sum() — values weighted by their sample counts. That last small batch barely moves the correct average, but it yanks the naive one around.
