From a stack of feature maps to the Gram matrix — one cell at a time

A VGG layer hands style loss a stack of C feature maps (here 6 toy maps, 8×8). For each table cell the recipe pulls two maps out of the stack, multiplies them cell-by-cell, averages the 64 products into one number, and drops it into G. Press ▶ Play to watch every cell get built — or click two planes (or any G cell) to compute the pair you want. Drag to orbit.
