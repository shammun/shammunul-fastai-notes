Game of Life Lab — a world where every cell follows one tiny rule

This grid is a tiny universe. Each square ("cell") is either alive (teal) or dead (white). Time moves in ticks called generations. At every tick, every cell looks at its 8 surrounding neighbors, counts how many are alive, and one rule table decides whether it lives or dies next. That's the entire program — no one is in charge, yet shapes glide, blink, and explode. This "local rules → global behavior" trick is exactly what our Neural CA is built on.

1. Click any cell on the grid (alive or dead — both are interesting).
2. Click the stage chips ① → ② → ③ → ④ in order — the panel below narrates each step.
