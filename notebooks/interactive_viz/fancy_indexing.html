This is "fancy" (integer-array) indexing: give PyTorch two index lists of the same length and it zips them into coordinate pairs. The alternative — a Python for-loop over 50,000 samples — would be thousands of times slower. One vectorized gather, then mean and negate, and you have the NLL loss.
