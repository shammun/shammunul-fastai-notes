The UNet in 3D: watch one tensor travel through the small CIFAR-10 model

Thin panels = the model's blocks (click any for details + how init_ddpm treats it). The solid box is the data itself — a batch [bs, 3, 32, 32] — and it morphs at every block: face = the pixel grid (32×32 → 4×4 → 32×32), thickness = channels (3 → 256 → 3). The little gold dot is the timestep embedding riding along — injected into every block. On the way down, each block parks a copy on a shelf; on the way up, each copy flies back in and visibly fattens the box — that's a skip connection's torch.cat. Drag = rotate · scroll = zoom · ⏭ = one block · chips replay their part of the story.

How to read the scene
The orange box is the activation tensor. Its face is the spatial grid, its thickness is the channel count — so the encoder makes it smaller and fatter, and the decoder bigger and thinner again. Click any thin panel to inspect that block; the pills above trace every shape the tensor takes.
