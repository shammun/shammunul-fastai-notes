Inside a custom collate_fn: 4 samples become 1 batch

A DataLoader hands your collate function a list of samples — here, dicts of PIL image + integer label. The function's job is to turn that list into one neat batch: images [B,1,28,28] and labels [B]. Step through it, or press Play.

Why a custom collate? PyTorch's default collate can't turn PIL images into tensors, so we do it ourselves: TF.to_tensor converts + scales each image to [1,28,28] in 0–1, then torch.stack piles them up with a new batch axis in front.
