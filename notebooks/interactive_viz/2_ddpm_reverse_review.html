DDPM reverse process, reviewed — every symbol earns its place

DDPM’s reverse step comes from Bayesian inference: the posterior q(xt−1 | xt, x₀) of two Gaussians. Three tabs: the update rule piece by piece (with the number that explains why 1000 steps exist), the x̂₀ form — an interpolation, proven identical to the first form both by algebra and by arithmetic — and the three assumptions that chain DDPM to step-by-step sampling. 🎓 Where do those weights come from? Bayes’ rule: q(xt−1|xt, x₀) ∝ q(xt|xt−1) · q(xt−1|x₀). Both factors are Gaussians in xt−1; multiplying them and completing the square yields a Gaussian whose mean has exactly the two weights above and whose variance is β̃t. DDPM then replaces the unknown x₀ with x̂₀ — the same “honest step” from the theory-foundations page.
Skip-proofing check: which of DDPM’s three load-bearing assumptions breaks?
1 · Gaussian approximation
The reverse conditional is only approximately Gaussian — and the approximation is good precisely because each step is tiny. Left: for a 1-step hop the true distribution (orange) and the Gaussian DDPM assumes (dashed) coincide. Right: over a big hop the true conditional spreads and splits (illustration) — a single Gaussian visibly cannot represent it.
2 · Variance accumulation
Fresh noise must be re-injected in the right amount at every step. A hop of k needs the posterior variance over the whole gap; a DDPM step only ever supplies the 1-step amount β̃t. Drag the hop: 10 — the red shortfall is the broken variance the doc warns about.
3 · Markov chain
Each transition is conditioned only on the previous state — memoryless — and carries its own fresh z. The chain is only defined between adjacent timesteps: there simply is no DDPM transition kernel from t to t−10. All three properties point the same way: walk every step.
🔑 The escape hatch: DDIM rebuilds the reverse process as non-Markovian (each step consults x̂₀), with variance written directly in terms of the endpoint ᾱ’s — so assumptions 2 and 3 are simply not needed, and determinism (η=0) removes the per-step z entirely. That story is the companion page, DDIM’s key innovation.
