FID & KID: comparing distributions, not pixels

To score a generative model we never compare images one-by-one. Instead real images and generated images are both pushed through the same feature extractor, which turns each image into a 512-number vector. FID/KID then measure how far apart the two clouds of vectors sit. Click a stage chip to jump, click inside the pipeline, or press Play.
