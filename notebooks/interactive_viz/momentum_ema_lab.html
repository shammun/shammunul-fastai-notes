Momentum — average the gradients, kill the zigzag

Mini-batch gradients are noisy, and in narrow "ravine" losses plain SGD zigzags across the walls instead of moving along the floor. Momentum fixes both with one trick: keep an exponential moving average of gradients — grad_avg = mom·grad_avg + (1−mom)·grad — and step with the average.
