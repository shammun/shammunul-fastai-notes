Test-Time Augmentation — ask the model twice, average the answers

The model was trained on flipped images, so a flipped test image is just as "normal" to it. TTA exploits this: predict the original, predict the flipped copy, then average the two probability vectors and take the argmax. Two noisy opinions average into one better one — no retraining needed. Step through the stages, or pick a scenario to see when TTA rescues a wrong answer (and when it can't).
