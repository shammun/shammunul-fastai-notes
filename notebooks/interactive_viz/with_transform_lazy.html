Eager vs. lazy

An eager approach would convert all 8 samples (all 60,000 in real MNIST) to tensors up front — wasting time and memory on data you may never use.

Lazy .with_transform converts only what a DataLoader or loop actually pulls. Rows you never touch stay as compact PIL images.

.with_transform(fn) returns a view that keeps the original data in its compact on-disk (Arrow/PIL) form and applies fn only when a sample is read. This saves memory and startup time, and lets you swap transforms cheaply without recopying data.
