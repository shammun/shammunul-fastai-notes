Dropout — randomly silence neurons, scale up the survivors

Each training pass, every activation is kept with probability 1−p and zeroed with probability p — so no neuron can become indispensable. Survivors are scaled by 1/(1−p) so the layer's average output stays the same. Press ▶ Play to watch full passes run stage by stage — the code on the right highlights the exact line being executed. One pass wobbles around mean(x); the average over many passes converges to mean(x). That is what "the expectation is preserved" means — and it's exactly what ×1/(1−p) buys.
