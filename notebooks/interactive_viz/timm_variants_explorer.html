timm ResNet Family — what actually changes between variants?

Every ResNet is the same skeleton: stem ➜ 4 stages of residual blocks ➜ head (for a 224×224 image: 224 ➜ 56 ➜ 28 ➜ 14 ➜ 7 ➜ 1). Variants only turn three dials: blocks per stage, block type (BasicBlock vs Bottleneck), and stem/downsample tricks (the “d”). Pick a variant, then click any box — data flows left ➜ right, and top ➜ bottom inside each stage.
