The Learning Rate Finder — in 3D

The LR Finder trains for one batch at a time, multiplying the learning rate by ~1.3 after every batch. Each batch does a real gradient-descent step w ← w − lr·∇L on the loss surface below — so the step size literally grows with the LR. Watch the ball: nearly frozen at tiny LRs, descending cleanly in the sweet spot, then overshooting and bouncing until the loss diverges. Drag to orbit the surface; use Play to auto-step.

Two standard ways to read the curve: the steepest-descent point (fast.ai's default — where the smoothed loss falls fastest) and the minimum-loss ÷ 10 heuristic (Smith 2017). Both sit safely left of the divergence. In LRFinderCB, after_batch raises CancelFitException once the smoothed loss exceeds ~3× its best value — which is exactly where the ball here starts blowing up.
