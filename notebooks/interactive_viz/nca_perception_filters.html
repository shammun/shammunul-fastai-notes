Perception Filter Lab — how each NCA cell "sees" its neighborhood

Before the "brain" can think, each cell perceives its 3×3 neighborhood through 4 fixed filters: Identity (my own value), Sobel X (change left→right), Sobel Y (change top→bottom), Laplacian (am I different from my surroundings?). Pick a filter and a pattern, press ▶, and click any output cell to see the exact arithmetic. Toggle circular padding to see the wrap-around trick.
