The Callback System: Hooks Firing in Order

At each hook point the Learner calls run_cbs(cbs, "before_batch"), which loops over every callback sorted by .order and runs its matching method — if it has one. Step through to watch each hook fire the right callback methods. Click a hook to jump there.

This is the whole trick: the Learner never imports or knows any callback. It just shouts a hook name; run_cbs delivers it to whoever is listening, lowest order first. A callback can even shout back by raising a Cancel exception to skip the rest of a batch, epoch, or the whole fit.
