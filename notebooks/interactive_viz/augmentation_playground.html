The Augmentation Machine — RandomCrop(padding) + RandomHorizontalFlip

Every training batch, each image goes through the same pipeline: ① pad the 28×28 image with zeros → ② pick a random top-left corner → ③ crop 28×28 back out → ④ maybe flip left↔right. The model never sees exactly the same pixels twice. Click stage chips, click the padded image to choose the crop corner yourself, or press Play to watch endless variants stream out.
