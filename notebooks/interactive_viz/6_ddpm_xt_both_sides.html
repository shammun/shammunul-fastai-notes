Why is x_t on both sides?Python variable reuse — the right side is read first (old xt), then the box is overwritten with xt−1.

      
What equation is it?The DDPM posterior mean + noise (the boxed formula on tab ③).

      
Where does it come from?Bayes’ theorem on the Gaussian forward process: q(xt−1 | xt, x₀).

      
Intuition?A cautious, trust-weighted blend of x̂₀ and xt, plus fresh noise — repeated ~1000×.
