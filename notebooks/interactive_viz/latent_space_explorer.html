Drag the purple marker (or click a cluster) and watch the decoder rebuild an image from that point. Move between clusters and the reconstruction morphs smoothly — proof the code is meaningful, not just memorized.

The marker's position is a 2-D stand-in for the 256-number latent code. The decoder turns whatever point you pick into a 28×28 image.

Land inside a cluster → a clean, confident reconstruction. Land between clusters → a blurry blend, because that region wasn't seen much in training.

Smooth morphing between clusters is the signature of a well-organized latent space — the whole reason autoencoders are useful for compression and feature learning.
