One-pixel audit — the algebra as arithmetic
We have x̂₀. How do we get xt−1?
This is the exact point where DDPM and DDIM part ways. Both re-mix the estimate back to noise level t−1 — they differ in one term.
DDPM — re-mix, then add FRESH noise
xt−1 = √ᾱt−1 · x̂₀
+ √(β̄t−1 − σ²) · εθ
+ σt · z ,  z ~ N(0, I)  ← fresh!
DDIM (η=0) — re-mix, reuse ε_θ, no noise
xt−1 = √ᾱt−1 · x̂₀
+ √β̄t−1 · εθ  ← same εθ, reused
σ = 0 → no random term at all
