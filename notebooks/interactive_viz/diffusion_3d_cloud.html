Every image is a point — diffusion in data space (3D)

Imagine each training image squashed to a point in space (real images live in ~1000-dimensional space; here it is 3-D so we can see). The dataset forms a structured shape. Forward diffusion melts that shape into a featureless Gaussian ball; sampling is the learned walk back from the ball onto the shape. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click the chips to jump.
