μ and Σ: two numbers* that describe a whole cloud

FID never looks at individual feature vectors — only at each cloud's mean μ (its center) and covariance Σ (its spread, stretch and tilt). Here each cloud lives in 2-D so Σ is a tiny 2×2 matrix you can actually read. Drag a cloud to move its mean, use the sliders to reshape it, and hover the Σ entries to see what each one controls. (*OK — a 512-vector and a 512×512 matrix in the real notebook, but the idea is identical.)
